public GA decision is still red because the PDS deliberately requires evidence
that cannot be manufactured by code: human-approved launch inventory, moderated
Russian-language research, native assistive-technology/device runs, legal/store
approval, production key custody, and live operational ownership.

## Editorial readiness artifacts

The working Russian copy standard is versioned in
[`RUSSIAN_HOUSE_STYLE.md`](../content-style/RUSSIAN_HOUSE_STYLE.md), including
idiom use, `ты/вы`, `ё`, anti-calque and anti-cringe checks. Character-specific
calibration is captured by
[`CHARACTER_VOICE_BIBLE_TEMPLATE.md`](../content-style/CHARACTER_VOICE_BIBLE_TEMPLATE.md).
The automated screen also warns on common literal calques such as «я ценю это»,
«держать пространство» and «где мы стоим». These artifacts make review
repeatable; they do not turn generated fixture text into approved production
inventory.
